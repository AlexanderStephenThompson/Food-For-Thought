What separates two cuisines?

Every ingredient carries a weight for each cuisine. Subtract one cuisine's weights from another's and you get the ingredients that most tell them apart — straight from the model, no interpretation added.

Built from 39,774 Kaggle "What's Cooking" recipes. Differences are coefficient gaps — the model's own weights, subtracted.
