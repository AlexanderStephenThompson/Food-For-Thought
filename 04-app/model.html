How this model works — and where it fails

The blend is a calibrated probability, not a vibe

The model is multinomial logistic regression: every ingredient carries one learned weight per cuisine. Sum the weights of a dish's ingredients, run them through a softmax, and you get a probability for each cuisine. The temperature step rescales those probabilities so a stated 70% means roughly 70% in practice. An ingredient's contribution you see elsewhere on this site is exactly its value times its weight — the model's own arithmetic, nothing added.
